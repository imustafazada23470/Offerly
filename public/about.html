The idea behind Salesphere.

We built Salesphere to make it easier to find real discounts nearby, without wasting time jumping between different apps and stories.

What problem are we solving?

Every day, cafés, restaurants and cinemas share discounts on random channels: stories, posts, flyers, group chats. People who might love those offers simply never see them.

At the same time, students and locals spend a lot of time deciding where to go, trying to guess which place has a good price or an active offer. Salesphere brings these two sides together in one simple place.

What Salesphere does

  • Shows nearby discounts in a clean, searchable list instead of a million scattered posts.
  • Helps people quickly compare places by category, distance, and discount size.
  • Gives venues a lightweight way to get visibility without building their own app.

Who we are

Salesphere started as a small project to explore how technology can connect local businesses and people in a smarter way. It focuses on a simple, real-world use case: saving money while going out.
