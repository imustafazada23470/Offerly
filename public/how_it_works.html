How Salesphere works.

One place for nearby discounts. Simple for people who want to save money, and simple for venues that want more visitors.

For people who want to save

Salesphere helps you quickly decide where to go without opening ten different apps and stories.

1

Open Salesphere

Visit the website and set your area, or simply use the default city if you’re browsing from home.

2

Filter and compare

Use category filters and sorting to find the best discounts for cafés, restaurants, cinemas and more.

3

Go and show the deal

Once you choose a place, just go there and show the offer on your phone at the counter.

For businesses that want more guests

Instead of shouting into the void with random posts, you appear in front of people who are already looking for a place to go.

1

Share your details

Use the “For businesses” page to send us your venue name, type, area and contact email.

2

Agree on your discount

Decide which times and days you want to promote (for example, quiet weekday evenings).

3

Start appearing in searches

Your deals show up inside Salesphere when someone nearby filters by your category or area.
